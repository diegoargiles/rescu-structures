# R13 preliminary manufacturing and assembly plan

Each item is a feasible process concept, not a released drawing or supplier selection.

| Part | Proposed manufacture | Connection / open work |
|---|---|---|
| Round booms, OD25/ID23 x470mm | Purchased roll-wrapped CFRP tube with axial and bias reinforcement; cut and finish ends | Verify actual layup, wall tolerance, straightness, bending/torsion properties and local ovalization; do not assume pultruded axial stock has adequate torsion |
| Motor pods | Separately molded composite socket/deck/web part, bonded to tube using indexed jig | CAD assumes1.6g/cm3; final fiber architecture, radii, bond length/fillets and local fastener hardpoints TBD; motor remains bolted and replaceable |
| Wing sockets | Molded composite sleeve with reinforced clevis ears, bonded to boom | Existing small rib bolts retained; compressive/bearing strength, laminate details, bushing/washer/isolation and access need review |
| Aft wing tubes | Small CFRP tube, candidate OD5/ID3.5, swept from root socket to beyond Y410 rib | Candidate size only, stiffness/strength not sized; angled socket and smooth load introduction need local review |
| Aft root sockets / collars | Molded or machined GFRP parts; bond with controlled gap | Metal D3 root pin remains removable; define pin retention, bearing length, effective bond area and skin/rib doublers |
| Foam ribs | Cut Ravatherm XPS to existing profiles; drill/router clearance with jig | Holes alone do not transfer torque. Bonded collars distribute loads; foam bearing, skin connection and reinforcement remain open |
| ESC deck and lower cradles | Cut GFRP sheet/deck, fitted bonded cradles | Add accessible ESC straps/fasteners, electrical isolation, thermal path/airflow and harness strain relief after selected ESC details |
| Main carry-through | Retain R12 bonded-support direction | Detail sleeve/rail/frame bond overlaps, skin load spreading and accessible M3 wing retention in next joint revision |

## Assembly intent

1. Dry-fit and fixture fuselage supports and ESC cradle positions before bonding. Inspect bond surfaces and access.
2. Assemble each wing's spars, root sockets and reinforced ribs before closing skin. Verify dry-fit pin/joiner alignment at0deg wing datum.
3. Bond motor pods and wing sockets to each boom in a jig that controls spacing, motor vertical axes and angular indexing. Keep motor hardware removable.
4. Bolt boom sockets to reinforced wing rib; route phase leads with abrasion protection and strain relief. Install accessible wing-root electrical disconnects.
5. Slide wing onto straight retained joiner and rear anti-rotation pin; install retention screws under access covers.
6. Check geometry under representative deflection before accepting propeller clearance. Inspect sample bonds and representative joint tests before release.

## Evidence still required

Supplier material/layup data or coupons; adhesive compatibility and preparation; bond shear/peel; bolt bearing/pullout; tube local buckling/ovalization; wing torque transfer; wire mass/cooling; tool/hand access; dimensional drawing tolerances. CAD pods are separate components, not evidence of a cured laminate. No final manufacturing cost, drag reduction or flight-strength claim.
